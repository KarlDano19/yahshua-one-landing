PayrollHero is built for biometric attendance verification across shift-based, multi-branch teams, mostly retail and food and beverage chains. YAHSHUA One is built for unified payroll, HR, and compliance, with AI that reads your actual company data. Which one fits depends on what your real bottleneck is, not which one has more features.

## What PayrollHero Is Actually Built For

PayrollHero's core product is selfie and facial-recognition clock-in, built to verify that the right employee is at the right location at the right time across many physical branches.

Its own product pages are organized around this: selfie clock-in, schedules, attendance resolution, worksites, and a dedicated page for restaurants specifically. Payroll is listed as an add-on to that attendance core, not the product's starting point. Its customer list reflects this directly: Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf, Krispy Kreme, Subway Philippines, and Faburrito Restaurants are named on PayrollHero's own customer page, all multi-location, shift-based operations where verifying who actually clocked in matters more than anything else.

## What YAHSHUA One Is Actually Built For

YAHSHUA One is built to unify payroll, HR, and accounting for Filipino SMBs, with an AI assistant, Theo, that reads the company's actual configured data to answer questions and take permission-aware actions.

The starting point is different: instead of attendance verification for a distributed shift workforce, YAHSHUA One is built around getting payroll computation, statutory contributions, and BIR compliance right, with HR and accounting data flowing through the same system instead of three separate tools. Theo can answer a specific question about your own configured setup using your real numbers, not a general knowledge base, and generate payroll formulas in plain language for users unfamiliar with formula syntax.

## YAHSHUA One vs. PayrollHero: Side by Side

The two products solve different starting problems, which shows clearly once you compare them feature by feature instead of by marketing category.

## Which One Fits Your Business

If your actual bottleneck is verifying attendance for shift workers scattered across many physical locations, PayrollHero's biometric system was built specifically for that problem, and it shows in which customers use it.

If your bottleneck is payroll accuracy, statutory compliance, or the manual work of keeping payroll, HR, and accounting data in sync across separate tools, that's the problem YAHSHUA One starts from. Neither is a universally better product. They're built to solve different problems first, and the right choice depends on which one is actually costing you time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is PayrollHero actually built for?**
Biometric and selfie-based attendance verification for shift-based, multi-branch teams, mainly retail and food and beverage chains with hourly staff across many locations. Payroll is an add-on module on top of that core attendance product, not the starting point.

**How much does PayrollHero cost?**
PayrollHero's own pricing page lists $3.50 per employee per month for HRIS, timekeeping, attendance, and scheduling, plus a $3.00 per employee per month add-on for payroll and a $2.00 per employee per month add-on for leave management. A full setup with payroll runs about $6.50 per employee per month, priced in US dollars.

**Does PayrollHero have an AI assistant?**
Not that's documented on their own site as of this writing. Their product pages cover selfie clock-in, scheduling, attendance resolution, analytics, and HRIS, but no AI copilot or assistant feature is listed anywhere in their product menu.

**Is YAHSHUA One better than PayrollHero?**
Depends on your actual bottleneck. If it's verifying attendance for shift workers across many physical locations, PayrollHero's biometric system is built specifically for that. If it's payroll accuracy, statutory compliance, and unifying HR, payroll, and accounting data with AI that reads your real numbers, that's what YAHSHUA One is built for.

**Can I switch from PayrollHero to YAHSHUA One?**
Yes. The same year-to-date data continuity rules apply to any mid-year payroll switch, regardless of which provider you're moving from. See our guide to switching payroll providers mid-year for what to check before you migrate.
