Payroll software in the Philippines is not one product category. It is compliance software, HR software, and now AI software, sold under one label. Before comparing vendors, decide what you are actually buying: statutory compliance depth, data privacy handling, a real cost at your headcount, and whether any "AI" feature reads your actual payroll data or just talks about payroll in general.

## How to Choose Payroll Software in the Philippines

The right choice depends on five checks, in this order: statutory compliance depth, data privacy posture, real cost at your specific headcount, migration support, and whether the AI is grounded in your data or generic.

**Statutory compliance depth.** Every serious vendor claims SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR compliance. Ask instead how the system handles a mid-year salary increase, a resigned employee's final pay, or a maternity leave differential under RA 11210. Those edge cases separate a system that computes correctly from one that computes the easy 90% correctly.

**Data privacy posture.** Payroll data is some of the most sensitive information a business holds. Before signing up, check whether the vendor is registered with the National Privacy Commission and how it describes its Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) compliance. Almost none of the major Philippine payroll comparison guides currently ask this question. It is worth asking anyway.

**Real cost at your headcount.** A per-employee rate that looks cheap at 10 people can be expensive at 80. A flat rate that looks expensive at 10 people can be cheap at 80. Run the actual math for your team size, not the marketing number.

**Migration support.** Ask specifically what happens to your SSS and PhilHealth remittance history, your BIR Form 2316 year-to-date figures, and your existing payroll records when you switch. A vendor that cannot answer this clearly is not ready for your onboarding.

**Grounded AI, not generic AI.** Ask any AI feature a specific question about your own configured setup during a demo, not a general compliance question it could answer from public information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does payroll software cost in the Philippines?**
Published rates range from about PHP 77 per employee per month (GreatDay HR, 50-license minimum) to roughly $6.50 per employee per month for PayrollHero's combined HRIS and payroll tiers. Sprout's outsourced Payroll Starter plan is a flat PHP 7,900 per month for up to 10 employees, with its software-only tiers quoted per business. YAHSHUA One Payroll also prices by quote, not a public list.

**Why don't some payroll vendors publish their prices?**
Because payroll pricing depends on headcount, modules, and how much statutory complexity a business actually has. A quote-based model is not automatically a red flag. What matters is whether the vendor gives you a firm number quickly, in one call, or drags the process out with sales gatekeeping.

**Does payroll software in the Philippines handle SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR automatically?**
Most established Philippine payroll platforms compute these correctly. The real differences show up in edge cases: mid-year salary changes, resignations, maternity leave differentials, and how quickly the vendor updates its system when BIR or SSS changes a rate. Ask for a specific example, not a feature-list checkmark.

**What should I check before switching payroll providers?**
Confirm your remittance history and year-to-date figures (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR Form 2316) can be exported and migrated, not just employee master data. A provider switch mid-year is a statutory continuity problem first and a software problem second.

**Is AI in payroll software just a chatbot, or does it do more?**
It depends on the vendor. Some AI features are a support chatbot layered on top of the same manual system. Others are built to read the company's actual configured payroll data and answer questions grounded in it. Ask a vendor's AI a specific question about your own setup during a demo. A generic answer means it is not actually reading your data.
